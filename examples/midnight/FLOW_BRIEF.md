# FLOW BRIEF: "Midnight" (briefcut showcase)

Four 8 s clips, 9:16, one video model for all of them. The film opens on the loudest, tightest
action (a hook in the first second), ends on the midnight strike. Target film length 15-20 s.

## Shared style (appended to every prompt)

Night, a small old clockmaker's workshop, a single warm tungsten work lamp, deep shadows, dust in the
lamp light, brass and dark walnut tones, faint blue moonlight from a window. Photorealistic,
cinematic, 35 mm, shallow depth of field. No people's faces, no text, no captions, no logos.
Vertical 9:16.

## Scenes

### S1_HAMMER (hook)
Extreme macro close-up on a workbench under the lamp. Weathered old hands in rolled linen sleeves
hold a small brass gear on a steel shaft and strike it with a tiny brass hammer: three short, hard,
distinct taps, the first one almost immediately at the very start, then about one second apart.
Each tap clearly hits the gear, small metal dust sparkles in the lamp light. Camera locked off.

### S2_GEARS
Extreme macro of an open brass clock movement. Tweezers lower one final small gear onto its post
early in the shot, it drops and meshes. Right after, the balance wheel starts oscillating and the
gears begin to advance in small, visible, regular steps, about two steps per second, until the end.
Lamp glints on the brass. Camera locked off.

### S3_CUCKOO
Medium shot of a carved dark walnut Black Forest cuckoo clock on a dark wooden wall, side lamp light.
About one second in, the small wooden door snaps open and a carved wooden bird pops out, bows, goes
back in, and pops out a second time, then the door snaps shut. Pine cone weights sway. Camera locked off.

### S4_MIDNIGHT
A wall covered floor to ceiling with dozens of antique wall clocks, brass pendulums. Start close on the
largest central clock, white enamel face, Roman numerals; its minute hand moves the last step and
meets the hour hand exactly at XII around two seconds in. At that instant every pendulum on the wall
swings together in unison and the lamp flickers once. Slow pull-back to reveal the whole wall, hold.

## Takes

Two takes per scene. Best take per scene goes to `clips/S1_HAMMER.mp4` ... `clips/S4_MIDNIGHT.mp4`,
rejected takes to `clips/_rejected/`. Native audio is discarded by the pipeline.
